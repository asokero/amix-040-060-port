WHAT THIS RUN CAN AND CANNOT DECIDE
------------------------------------
It CANNOT decide ISSUE-53.  The defect needs an SDMAC interrupt that is not a WD interrupt,
and this emulator never produced one: a3w_or_istr, the OR of every entry snapshot across
131538 level-2 interrupts, is 0x00d1 -- bit 5 (E_INT) is NEVER set, not once, in any
combination.  The mechanism the wrapper exists to catch does not occur here at all.

It CAN decide four things hardware should not be spent on:
  1. the wrapper is actually in int2_tbl and its body runs (a3w_ran, and a nonzero denominator);
  2. it classifies real traffic correctly and the buckets are mutually exclusive;
  3. it does not break the disk -- 29928 A3091 interrupts served, byte-exact file truth;
  4. it never takes the loud path: a3w_other = 0, and nothing was printed to console or serial.

READ SKEW, MEASURED RATHER THAN ASSUMED
----------------------------------------
Earlier reads of the same three counters gave sums exceeding a3w_calls by 2, 18, 4 and 34.
The gap VARIES, which is what distinguishes read skew from an off-by-N defect: kpeek does
one lseek+read per longword, a3w_calls is read first and is therefore the stalest, and the
machine keeps taking level-2 interrupts between the reads.  The final read caught a quiet
moment and reconciled exactly.  Same effect recorded for the fpc block on 2026-08-12.

Consequence for the hardware run: do not treat a small positive gap on invariant 1 as a
failure.  Only a NEGATIVE gap (sum < calls) would be a defect, and no read produced one.
